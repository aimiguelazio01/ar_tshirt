## Brand & Style

The design system bridges avant-garde street/techwear apparel with high-end VFX pipeline utilities. Built for an elite audience of digital artists, technical directors, streetwear collectors, and WebAR pioneers, the visual language merges the technical precision of 3D DCC software (viewports, node graphs, camera frustums) with brutalist luxury garment tagging.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **VFX Studio Telemetry:** Interfaces mirror unrendered camera passes, point clouds, and precision optics. Data readouts are authoritative, monospaced, and strictly aligned.
- **Cyber-Tactile Physicality:** High-contrast obsidian planes accented by brushed titanium surfaces, raw hairline dividers, and electric luminous state changes.
- **Subtle Industrial Restraint:** Function precedes decoration. Visual density is balanced by deep obsidian voids, razor-thin structural borders, and purposeful neon focal points.
- **Dynamic State Projection:** Luminescent states (Beast Amber, Maya Cyan, Magma Red) indicate tracking status, AR surface recognition, and raycast lock without unnecessary chrome.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for darkened 3D viewports and physical WebAR camera overlays. Contrast meets strict AAA legibility standards against deep obsidian while preserving optical punch through selective spectral accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **BaseVoid (`#08080C`):** Absolute viewport ground. Absorbs reflections, isolates viewport elements, and mimics ambient light studio calibration screens.
- **PanelSurface (`#12141C`):** Base structural container for floating HUD cards, viewport bottom sheets, and hardware calibration panels.
- **PanelHighlight (`#1C1F2C`):** Interactive resting states, hovered telemetry rows, and toggle well backgrounds.
- **HairlineBorder (`#272B3C`):** 1px structural division lines defining industrial panels and reticle enclosures.
- **TitaniumSilver (`#D0D5DE`):** Primary display text, precision iconography, active sliders, and tactile shutter hardware.
- **TextMuted (`#7E8597`):** Coordinate axes, matrix indexes, field labels, units of measurement, and unselected states.

### Status & State Triad
- **BeastAmber (`#FF9E00`):** Core brand energy. Drives primary action buttons, NFC polling cycles, searching reticles, and unresolved raycast pings.
- **MayaCyan (`#00C9B7`):** Spatial confirmation, stable SLAM surface anchors, AR plane index 01, and successful pipeline imports.
- **MagmaRed (`#FF3D00`):** Critical alerts, depth occlusion dropouts, dropped frames, unsupported WebXR hardware, and AR plane index 02.
- **CyberPurple (`#7C4DFF`):** Gesture tracker handles, procedural geometry nodes, secondary tags, and AR plane index 03.
- **VoltYellow (`#FFF275`):** Sub-telemetry highlights, lighting estimation warnings, exposure clipping, and AR plane index 04.

## Typography

The typographic system communicates hierarchy through contrast of geometry and purpose:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headers):** Delivers aggressive, techwear-inspired forms. Headings are compact, wide-set, and authoritative.
2. **Inter (Body & Dialogs):** Handles rapid human comprehension inside settings trays, modal explainers, and instructional tooltips.
3. **JetBrains Mono (HUD Telemetry & Spatial Readouts):** Exclusively uppercase, widely spaced, and engineered for live AR coordinates `[X, Y, Z]`, tracking frequencies, frame timings, and hardware status flags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is treated as a camera lens overlay: content floats across viewport layers with strict edge adherence and safe zone awareness for mobile notch/home indicators.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Base Grid:** Fixed 8pt baseline system with micro 4pt steps for telemetry clustering.
- **AR Viewport Clear Zone:** Center 60% of mobile viewports is designated zero-intrusion space for point clouds, spatial anchoring, and gesture reticles.
- **HUD Edge Anchorage:**
  - **Top Rail:** Session status, camera FPS, tracking stability, settings gear.
  - **Bottom Deck:** Persistent shutter hub, tool selector, transformation axis toggles.
  - **Lateral Wings:** Depth visualizers, transform gizmo values, and NFC state tags.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `compact` (< 640px): Dynamic HUD docks, swipe-up drawer sheets, vertical camera rail.
  - `expanded` (>= 640px): Fixed lateral floating modules, split telemetry cards, pinned node inspectors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built through industrial layering rather than conventional drop shadows. Shadows are crisp, tinted, and minimal; primary depth is governed by background blur and edge illumination.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Viewport Ground):** The raw WebGL/WebXR camera feed backed by `#08080C`.
- **Level 1 (Telemetry Panels & Dock Containers):** `#12141C` with `90%` opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bound by a 1px solid `#272B3C` border.
- **Level 2 (HUD Floating Pills & Action Modals):** `#1C1F2C` with `95%` opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, encased in a dual border (1px `#272B3C` outer, 1px inset `#D0D5DE` at 8% opacity). Ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(8, 8, 12, 0.75)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused Overlays & System Dialogs):** `#12141C` solid, framed by structural corner brackets with an active highlight glow: `0 0 24px rgba(255, 158, 0, 0.15)`.
- **Spectral Overdrive (Locked Target):** Interactive spatial elements project an optical rim glow using `0 0 16px rgba(0, 201, 183, 0.40)`.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `1` (0.25rem base radius), the geometry enforces a refined, precision-milled appearance reminiscent of high-end CNC hardware, camera bodies, and physical techwear badges.

### Spatial Geometries
- **Base Elements:** Standard cards, panels, inputs, and chips utilize `rounded` (4px).
- **Secondary Groupings:** Outer HUD containers use `rounded-lg` (8px) to establish subtle exterior softness without compromising rigidity.
- **Corner Notches & Reticles:** Select elements (such as targeting crosshairs and telemetry cards) employ chamfered clip paths (`polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 8px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) to express industrial utility.
- **Optical Circularity:** Shutter buttons, hardware dials, and NFC pulse indicators break the linear grid with pure 50% circular geometry, evoking optical lenses.

## Components

- **Primary Action (Beast Shutter):** Circular 68px trigger. Outer ring is 2px solid `#D0D5DE` spaced by 3px from an inner solid `#FF9E00` disc. On press, scale down to 0.94 with an amber pulse ring expanding outward.
- **Secondary Action (HUD Action):** Background `#1C1F2C`, text `#D0D5DE`, border 1px `#272B3C`. Height 36px, `Space Grotesk` 14px bold, uppercase. On hover: border `#FF9E00`, text `#FF9E00`.
- **Ghost Tool:** Height 32px, no background, border 1px transparent, icon/text `#7E8597`. On active: background `#1C1F2C`, text `#00C9B7`, border 1px `#00C9B7`.

### 2. HUD Telemetry Pills & Chips
- **Status Indicator:** Height 24px, background `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.85)`, backdrop blur 12px, border 1px `#272B3C`, radius 4px. Left-anchored status LED dot (6px) with breathing state animation (`#FF9E00` searching, `#00C9B7` tracking locked). Right content in `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase `#D0D5DE`.
- **Toggle Chip:** Height 28px, background `#12141C`. When inactive, border 1px `#272B3C` and text `#7E8597`. When active, background `rgba(124, 77, 255, 0.15)`, border 1px `#7C4DFF`, text `#D0D5DE`.

### 3. Reticle Brackets & Spatial Anchors
- **Targeting Crosshair:** Four detached 8px corner brackets around a 48px bounding area. Line weight 1.5px `#FF9E00`. Center point alternates between a 2px crosshair and coordinates readouts (`JetBrains Mono` 9px).
- **Surface Confirmation Plane:** Wireframe grid projection fading to 0% opacity at perimeter, dynamic color mapping across planes: Plane 01 `#00C9B7`, Plane 02 `#FF3D00`, Plane 03 `#7C4DFF`, Plane 04 `#FFF275`.

### 4. Cards & Modals
- **Telemetry Drawer:** Slide-up container with `#12141C` background, top border 1px `#272B3C`, drag handle styled as an engraved mechanical slot (32px × 3px `#272B3C`). Headers in `Space Grotesk`, data values right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono`.
- **High-Tech Dialog:** Centered panel flanked by micro-corner index ticks (`+` markers at corners in `#7E8597`). Contains an authoritative title, monospace diagnostic code, and an affirmative amber button spanning full width.

### 5. Input Fields & Parameter Controls
- **Precision Slider:** 2px track in `#272B3C`, filled track in `#FF9E00`, thumb is a vertical 6px × 14px titanium block (`#D0D5DE`) with twin grip notches. Numerical output pinned above in `JetBrains Mono` 11px `#D0D5DE`.
- **Coordinate Stepper:** Segmented card with inline `[-]` and `[+]` tactile buttons bounding a monospaced readout. Border 1px `#272B3C`, background `#08080C`.

### 6. Lists & Data Rows
- **Inspector Row:** Height 36px, flex row with border-bottom 1px `#272B3C`. Label in `Inter` 13px `#7E8597`, telemetry data in `JetBrains Mono` 12px `#D0D5DE`. Hover reveals an inline `#FF9E00` accent tick on the left border.
